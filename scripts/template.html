Nasdaq Signal Board

A rules-based model built from the ideas leading quant funds publish and practise: trend following, 12-month momentum, relative strength, short-term mean reversion and volatility-based risk control. Stocks are bought, held or avoided, never shorted. Shorts are taken only on the Nasdaq-100 and S&P 500, as a hedge when the broad market is in a downtrend.

Index shorts (hedge)

Rulebook

How a signal is decided

Educational research tool, not investment advice. Shorting an index fund needs a margin account, and a short's loss has no ceiling if the market keeps rising; always use the stop. Signals use end-of-day prices from a public GitHub dataset (yennanliu/finance_data) that mirrors exchange closes; check prices with your broker before acting. Past backtest results do not predict future returns.
