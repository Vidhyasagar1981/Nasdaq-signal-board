Rulebook

How a signal is decided

Educational research tool, not investment advice. Short selling needs a margin account and shares available to borrow, and a short position's loss has no ceiling if the price keeps rising; always use the stop. Signals use end-of-day prices from a public GitHub dataset (yennanliu/finance_data) that mirrors exchange closes; check prices with your broker before acting. Past backtest results do not predict future returns.
